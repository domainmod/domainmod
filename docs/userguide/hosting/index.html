As of v4.27.0, DomainMOD keeps track of your hosting services, such as web hosting plans, servers, and email hosting, alongside your domains and SSL certificates. Each service belongs to a hosting account at a hosting provider, has a service type and a billing term, and gets its cost from a hosting fee. Your domains can each be linked to the service they use.

Getting Set Up

Before adding a service, add the hosting provider and your account there (see Creating Assets). DomainMOD comes with a set of service types (Cloud, Dedicated, DNS, Email, Other, Reseller, Shared, and VPS), and you can add your own under Assets -> Hosting Service Types.

Types are shared by every provider. A fee is set per provider, service type, and billing term, so if one provider sells two shared plans at different prices, add a type for each (such as Shared Starter and Shared Pro).

Adding a Hosting Service

  1. Click Hosting on the main menu.

  2. Click the Add Hosting Service button.

  3. Enter the service's Name, and choose its Hosting Account (the owner comes from the account), Service Type, Billing Term (1, 3, 6, 12, 24, or 36 months), and Status.

  4. Enter the Renewal Date, and choose whether Auto-Renewal is on. Renews On Day is the day of the month it renews on, so if a shorter month moves a renewal earlier, the next one goes back to that day.

  5. Under Where It Runs, you can enter the Hostname and choose an IP Address. Choose a Category, and add any Notes.

  6. Click the Add Hosting Service button.

Fees

A service's cost comes from the hosting fee that matches its provider, service type, and billing term. To add one, click Assets on the main menu, click Hosting Providers under Hosting, then click Fees beside the provider, and click Add Fee.

A fee has an Initial Fee (for your records, and not counted in costs), a Renewal Fee for each billing term, and an optional Misc Fee that's added to every renewal, such as a control panel license. When you add or change a fee, the services that match it are updated.

A service with no matching fee is saved without a cost and shows No fee in the list, and it's left out of cost totals until you add one. Reporting -> Hosting Fees lists the fees that are missing. The Hosting list's Yearly Cost is each service's cost per billing term scaled to 12 months.

The Hosting Page

The Hosting page lists your live hosting services (Active and Pending) to start with. You can filter it by Hosting Provider, Hosting Account, Service Type, Billing Term, IP Address, Category, Owner, and Status, and by renewal date. Export downloads the list as a CSV file, and you can choose its columns in Settings -> Display Settings.

Click a service to open its page, where you can edit its details and see its Change History. Its Domains section lists the domains that use it, each with its expiry date and status.

Linking Domains

A domain's add and edit pages have a Hosting Service menu, under Hosting. Choose a service, or None. Choosing a service also fills in the domain's IP Address with the service's (on a domain you're editing, only if its IP address isn't already set), which you can still change, since a domain can point somewhere else (such as through a proxy).

To link many domains at once, use the Bulk Updater's Change Hosting Service action. The Domains list can show a Hosting Service column, and its Hosting Service filter finds the domains on a service, or with none.

Renewals

When a service renews, open it and click Mark Renewed, which moves its renewal date forward one billing term.

Once a service's renewal date has passed, it shows one of these:

  • Confirm renewal: the service is Active and auto-renew is on, so the host should have renewed it. Check that it did, then choose Mark Renewed. After 14 days, it shows as overdue.
  • Overdue: auto-renew is off, the service is Pending, or it waited more than 14 days for confirmation.

Hosting services are included in the expiration email, the Dashboard, the Renewal Calendar, and the reports, including Hosting Renewals and At-Risk Hosting Services.

Cancelled Services

To stop tracking a service you no longer have, set its Status to Cancelled. A cancelled service is hidden from the list unless you filter by status, has no renewals, and is left out of costs. Domains already linked to it keep the link, but it can't be chosen for other domains.

A service can be deleted from the bottom of its page, and any linked domains are kept without a hosting service. Hosting providers, accounts, and service types can only be deleted once nothing uses them.

Custom Hosting Fields

Administrators can add custom fields for hosting services in Administration -> Custom Hosting Fields. See Custom Fields.
