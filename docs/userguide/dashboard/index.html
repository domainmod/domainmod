The Dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It gives you an overview of your domains, hosting services, and SSL certificates, and what needs your attention.

The Dashboard Page

  • Totals: how many domains, hosting services, SSL certificates, and sold domains you have.
  • Needs Attention: anything at risk, such as domains expiring soon with auto-renewal off, or hosting renewals waiting to be confirmed.
  • Coming Up: the renewals due in the next 7 and 30 days, and within the Expiration Days to Display setting in Administration -> System Settings (60 by default), including overdue ones.
  • Pending: how many domains, hosting services, and SSL certificates have each Pending status.
  • Renewal Costs, Next 12 Months: a chart of what your renewals will cost each month. Click Show the figures to see them as a table.

Each number links to the list or report behind it. Administrators also see a warning here when the Task Scheduler has stopped running.

The Getting Started Checklist

On a new installation, the Dashboard shows a Getting Started checklist, which walks you through adding your registrar, your registrar account, your fees, and your domains. Administrators also get a step for setting up the cron job. Each step ticks itself off as you go, and the checklist goes away once you've added your first domain, hosting service, or SSL certificate.

If you'd rather not see it, click Hide this checklist. You can turn it back on in Settings -> Display Settings.
