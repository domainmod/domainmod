Segments are saved lists of domains that you can filter the Domains list by, such as the domains used by one client or project, or a list you've been sent to check. The domains don't have to be in DomainMOD, so a segment also shows you which ones are missing.

Adding a Segment

  1. Click Segments on the main menu.

  2. Click the Add Segment button.

  3. Enter a Segment Name (up to 35 characters), and paste in the Segment Domains, one per line.

  4. Add a Description and any Notes, if you'd like.

  5. Click the Add Segment button.

If any of the domains aren't valid, DomainMOD lists them so you can fix them, and the segment isn't saved until they're fixed.

Editing a Segment

On the Segments page, click a segment's name to change its name, domains, description, or notes, then click Save. To delete a segment, click Delete this segment in the Danger Zone at the bottom of the page. Deleting a segment doesn't affect the domains in it.

Filtering the Domains List

On the Domains page, click Filters, choose a segment from the Segment menu, and click Apply filters. The list then shows only the domains in the segment, and you can use the other filters with it, such as Registrar or Status. A summary above the list shows:

  • Domains in Segment: how many domains the segment has
  • Matching Domains: how many of them are on the list, and their Total Cost
  • Matching But Inactive Domains: domains that are in DomainMOD but are Expired or Sold
  • Matching But Filtered Domains: active domains that the list's other filters leave out
  • Missing Domains: domains that aren't in DomainMOD at all

The last three are only shown when they have any domains, and each one's View link lists them, with an Export Results button that downloads them as a CSV file. The segment is shown above the list with the other filters in use, where you can remove it.

Exporting

  • On the Segments page, the Export button downloads every segment and its domains as a CSV file, and the Export link beside a segment downloads just that one.
  • On the Domains page, Export downloads the domains the list is showing, with the segment's summary at the top.
